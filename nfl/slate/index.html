How we build these numbers

  • Lines: de-vigged prediction-market prices fit to spread, total and team-total ladders; a consensus line where no market trades yet.
  • Projections: our drive-by-drive game simulator anchored to the market totals and player markets, blended with recent form. p10-p90 is the middle 80% of 10,000 simulated games. Injury tags are hedged until an official report.
  • Ownership: our own model, trained on DraftKings contest results, with a weekly read of where the field is leaning. Own % is projected large-field GPP ownership; SE % is single-entry contests, which run chalkier. Its levels are rough and it under-calls late, news-driven value.
  • Optimal % and leverage: Opt % is the share of our simulated slates in which a player lands in the optimal lineup; Lev = Opt % minus Own %.
  • Stacks: a quarterback and two of his pass catchers scored together in the same simulated games, so the correlation is built in.
  • Builds: model builds come from our rules-first optimizer; the simulator's top builds are our candidate lineups played against a simulated contest field built from our ownership. ROI, top-1% and cash rates are simulated, not results.
