3What won in games like this

  • Captain a wide receiver. In 16 past showdowns with a 3.5 to 6.5 spread and a total of 46 or more, a WR was the winning captain 56% of the time (QB 25%, TE 12%, RB 6%). The favorite supplied the captain 56%.
  • Mix both teams. Winners went 4-2 (41%) or 3-3 (34%) most often. Five from one team won only 5% of the time (41 winning lineups, 3.5 to 6.5 spreads).
  • The Buccaneers QB belongs in your lineup. 63% of winners had the underdog's quarterback somewhere, and 41% played both quarterbacks.
  • One kicker at most, defenses are optional. A kicker was in 41% of winners and a defense in 22%. Never two kickers, never a kicker at captain.
