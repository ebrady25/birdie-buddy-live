3What won in games like this

  • Captain a wide receiver. In 15 past showdowns with a 7 or more spread and a total of 45.5 or under, a WR was the winning captain 47% of the time (RB 27%, QB 13%, TE 13%). The favorite supplied the captain 67%.
  • Mix both teams. Winners went 4-2 (32%) or 3-3 (39%) most often. Five from one team won only 13% of the time (31 winning lineups, 7 or more spreads).
  • The Colts QB belongs in your lineup. 39% of winners had the underdog's quarterback somewhere, and 23% played both quarterbacks.
