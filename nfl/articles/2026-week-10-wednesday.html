Week 10 first look: Dallas holds the slate's best stack

SF@DAL's 55.8 total makes Dallas the stack to beat; the edge comes from Green Bay, KC@ATL and fading McCaffrey.

Wed Nov 11, 2026 · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-11-12 00:30 UTC

SF@DAL owns the slate's top total at 55.8, and the Dallas trio of Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb and Ryan Flournoy clears 75 DK points in 23% of our 10,000 sims, the best rate on the board. The field sees it too: Lamb projects at 37% owned. We build through that game and get different everywhere else.

TL;DR

  • Stack Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb and Ryan Flournoy: 23% of our sims clear 75 points, the slate's best, but expect a crowd.
  • For leverage, stack Jordan Love with Matthew Golden and Christian Watson; Love projects at just 2% owned.
  • Fade Christian McCaffrey at 28% projected ownership and play Javonte Williams, who lands in the optimal lineup more often in our sims.
  • Kyle Pitts Sr. costs $3,800 with a 19.9-point ceiling in KC@ATL, a shootout candidate the field underowns.

Games to attack

  • SF@DAL (55.8, our No. 1 environment): All four game flows land between 24% and 27% in market prices, so there's no safe side to fade. It's the slate's top-scoring game in 28% of our sims and reaches 60-plus combined points in 39%. Stack either side and bring it back.
  • CAR@NO (48.8, No. 2): A Carolina upset (31%) is the single likeliest flow, with a close game next (28%). Its quarterbacks draw 21% of projected QB ownership against a 14% top-game chance, so we take pieces, led by Chuba Hubbard as a bring-back, over the core stack.
  • BUF@NYJ (51.0, No. 3): The market gives Buffalo a 35% blowout chance behind a 29.8-point team total. Josh Allen has the slate's top QB ceiling (36.2) at 8% projected owned, the clearest way into this game.
  • Shootout watch: KC@ATL is the slate's top-scoring game in 11% of our sims, yet the field gives its quarterbacks just 7% of projected QB ownership. Over the last ten seasons, the week's highest-scoring game came from outside the top three totals in 56% of slates. It's worth a one-off lineup or a piece, not a core stack.

Stacks to build

1. Dak Prescott + CeeDee Lamb + Ryan Flournoy (DAL)

  • Numbers: 59.8 mean, 88.7 ceiling, and 23% of our 10,000 sims clear 75 points, for $20,500. Projected ownership: Prescott 10%, Lamb 37%, Flournoy 12%.
  • Bring-back + game flow: Christian McCaffrey lifts the four-player ceiling to 109.0 but adds 28% ownership; Keenan Allen (6%) is the quieter bring-back. Dallas has to keep pace for Prescott to throw late.
  • Why it wins a GPP: It's the best stack on the slate, and the field knows it: crowded. George Pickens is Out and Jake Ferguson is reported Out; not confirmed, so targets funnel to Lamb and Flournoy. The risk is a crowded top, so build the rest differently.

2. Jordan Love + Matthew Golden + Christian Watson (GB)

  • Numbers: 50.1 mean, 76.8 ceiling, 11% clear 75, for $21,500. Projected ownership: Love 2%, Golden 8%, Watson 8%.
  • Bring-back + game flow: Justin Jefferson (8%) takes the four-player ceiling to 95.3. The 42.1 total has to play up: MIN@GB is the slate's top-scoring game in 6% of our sims.
  • Why it wins a GPP: It has the third-best hit rate of any stack, and the game's quarterbacks draw 3% of QB ownership. The risk: Minnesota has allowed 10.2 DK points per game to QBs over 5 games, the fewest of any defense.

3. Trevor Lawrence + Parker Washington + Brenton Strange (JAX)

  • Numbers: 46.4 mean, 71.2 ceiling, 8% clear 75, for $16,900, the cheapest of the three. Projected ownership: Lawrence 9%, Washington 17%, Strange 5%.
  • Bring-back + game flow: Tony Pollard (2%) lifts the four-player ceiling to 85.2. With a 28.6-point team total, the stack hits when Lawrence throws the touchdowns before the lead turns into handoffs.
  • Why it wins a GPP: The low price leaves room for two stud running backs or Lamb. The risk is the 43% blowout chance itself: a big early lead can mean a fourth quarter of runs.
