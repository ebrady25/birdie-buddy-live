How we build these numbers

  • Lines and scripts: de-vigged prediction-market prices (Polymarket + Kalshi), fit to spread, total and team-total ladders. Script A–D probabilities come from the market's ±3.5 / ±13.5 lines where they trade.
  • Projections and ceilings: our drive-by-drive game simulator, built on nflverse play-by-play 2016–26, anchored to the market totals and player props, with injury and role news from nflverse and Sleeper. 10,000 simulated games per matchup. A stack's numbers come from the same simulated games, so the correlation is built in.
  • Ownership: our own model, trained on DraftKings contest results. Tiers only. It ranks players well, but its levels are rough, and it under-calls late, news-driven value.
  • Matchups: points allowed to each role by each defense, opponent-adjusted and shrunk toward zero. One season of this is mostly noise, so it only breaks ties.
  • The codex: 169 DraftKings Milly Maker winning lineups (2016–25) against 2,639 games. Winner rates are shown with k and n.
