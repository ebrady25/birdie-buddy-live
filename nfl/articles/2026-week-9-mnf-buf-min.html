Josh Allen is the chalk captain and still the leverage captain: our sims put him in the optimal captain spot 31% of the time against a projected 21% captain ownership. The crowd's bigger move is Carson Wentz at a projected 52% FLEX ownership, and that's the play we fade.

The game
