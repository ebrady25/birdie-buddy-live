TNF showdown: the field captains Green Bay; Carolina's upset is 26%

The three most popular captains are all Packers, but the market gives Carolina a 26% chance to win by 4+. McMillan and Hubbard are the way in.

Thu Oct 29, 2026 · CAR @ GB, kickoff Thu 8:15 PM ET · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-10-29 19:16 UTC

Green Bay is favored by 4, yet the market gives Carolina a 26% chance to win by 4+, more than a Packers blowout at 23%. The crowd doesn't care: the three most popular captains are all Packers, led by Jordan Love at a projected 15%.

TL;DR

  • Start with Tetairoa McMillan at captain: a projected 9% captain ownership, and Carolina wins by 4+ in 26% of outcomes.
  • Eat Jordan Love at FLEX: he projects at 68% owned, the top projection on the board at 17.8.
  • Fade Josh Jacobs at captain (projected 14%) for Chuba Hubbard, a 14.9-point projection at 9%.

The game

Green Bay is a 4-point favorite and wins 64% of the time in a deep market, with a 47 total split 25.5 to 21.6. Fav wins is the most likely flow at 28%, then Upset at 26%, Close at 24% and Blowout at 23%. Matthew Golden is Questionable with a 70% chance to play, and our showdown sim hasn't run yet, so there are no ceilings or optimal % here, only projections and ownership.

Captain picks

1. Tetairoa McMillan (CAR, WR)

  • Why: 13.4 points projected at a $14,100 captain salary, and he's Carolina's top receiver with Xavier Legette, Ja'seem Reed and Chris Brazzell II all Out. He wants Close or Upset, 50% combined. In our study of 41 showdown winners in games with a 3.5-6.5 point spread, 49% (k = 20) had a WR captain.
  • Crowd: a projected 9% captain ownership. Risk: 59% of those winning captains (k = 24) came from the favorite.

2. Jordan Love (GB, QB)

  • Why: the top projection at 17.8, at a $15,000 captain salary. He's the engine of a 25.5-point team total, and Fav wins plus Blowout cover 51% of outcomes.
  • Crowd: the most popular captain at a projected 15%. Risk: at the top price, an average night sinks him.

3. Chuba Hubbard (CAR, RB)

  • Why: 14.9 points projected, 0.4 behind Josh Jacobs, at a $13,500 captain salary. With Jonathon Brooks Out, the backfield is his, and a Close game keeps him fed.
  • Crowd: a projected 9% captain vs 14% for Jacobs. Risk: a Green Bay blowout takes the run game away.

Chalk we'll eat / chalk we'll fade

Eat

  • Jordan Love at a projected 68% FLEX is in two of every three lineups. Fading the top projection is a bet against a 25.5-point team total.

Fade

  • Josh Jacobs at captain, projected 14%. Pivot: Chuba Hubbard, projected 9% at captain for nearly the same points.

Bottom line

We start with McMillan at captain: the field stacks its captains on Green Bay, and Carolina wins or keeps it close in 50% of outcomes. Hubbard is the pivot off Jacobs. Check Golden before lock, because his 70% chance to play decides how Green Bay's targets split.

How we build these numbers

  • Line and game flows: de-vigged prediction-market prices (Polymarket + Kalshi). Blowout = favorite wins by 14+, Fav wins = by 4–13, Close = within 3, Upset = underdog wins by 4+.
  • Projections, ceilings and optimal %: our showdown simulator plays the game out in each of the four game flows, weighted by their market chances. Ceiling = about a 90th-percentile game; captain ceiling = 1.5x. Optimal % = how often a player is in the highest-scoring possible team, as captain or FLEX.
  • Ownership: our own model, trained on DraftKings showdown results, shown as projected % for large-field contests. It ranks players well, but its levels are rough, and it under-calls late, news-driven value.
  • The codex: our study of DraftKings showdown winning lineups (2018–25). Winner rates are shown with k and n.
