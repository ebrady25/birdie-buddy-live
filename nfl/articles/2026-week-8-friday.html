Week 8 final call: Dallas' 31-point total and a crowded stack

Dallas has the slate's best stack and the field knows it. We play it, then get different in Cincinnati and the late Rams game.

Fri Oct 30, 2026 · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-10-30 20:01 UTC

The field sees it too: Prescott projects at 20% owned (26% in single entry) and Lamb at 23%. We play the Dallas stack anyway and get different around it.

How we build these numbers

  • Lines and game flows: de-vigged prediction-market prices (Polymarket + Kalshi), fit to spread, total and team-total ladders. Game-flow chances come from the market's ±3.5 / ±13.5 lines where they trade.
  • Projections and ceilings: our drive-by-drive game simulator, built on nflverse play-by-play 2016–26, anchored to the market totals and player props, with public injury and role news. 10,000 simulated games per matchup. A stack's numbers come from the same simulated games, so the correlation is built in.
  • Ownership: our own model, trained on DraftKings contest results, shown as projected % for large-field GPPs (and single-entry contests where they differ) plus tiers by rank within position. It ranks players well, but its levels are rough, and it under-calls late, news-driven value.
  • Matchups: points allowed to each role by each defense, opponent-adjusted and shrunk toward zero. One season of this is mostly noise, so it only breaks ties.
  • The codex: 169 DraftKings Milly Maker winning lineups (2016–25) against 2,639 games. Winner rates are shown with k and n.
