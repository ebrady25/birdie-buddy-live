Week 9 first look: Dallas leads, and the field already knows it

Wed Nov 4, 2026 · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-11-05 01:32 UTC

Dallas and San Francisco are dead even at the top of Week 9: our skill-player projection puts DAL@IND at 189.2 points and LV@SF at 189.1. The field leans 49ers, with Brock Purdy the highest-owned QB at a projected 15% (19% in single entry). We start in Dallas, take the cheap way into the Rams game and save a low-owned stack for New Orleans.

TL;DR

  • Stack Dak Prescott with CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens: the slate's best trio, clearing 75 points in 15% of our sims.
  • Eat George Kittle chalk: he's in our optimal lineup 27% of the time, the highest rate on the slate.
  • Fade Greg Dulcich at 20% projected owned and pivot to Dalton Schultz or Evan Engram, both nearly unowned.
  • Get different with Tyler Shough, Chris Olave and Devaughn Vele: a 2%-owned QB on a New Orleans favorite.

Slate map

Three games hold most of the points. LV@SF has the top total at 53.0 and the slate's biggest team total, the 49ers at 31.6. DAL@IND (51.7) and DET@MIA (50.2) come next, with Dallas and Detroit both implied near 29. Kansas City is implied for 29.0 as well, but the Jets add little at 18.5.

Games to attack

  • DAL@IND (51.7, No. 1 in our projection): Fav wins is the most likely flow at 31%, and a 19% Indianapolis upset keeps both offenses throwing. Stack Dallas and bring it back with Jonathan Taylor.
  • LV@SF (53.0, No. 2): the market gives a 40% San Francisco blowout. That favors George Kittle as a one-off over a full Purdy stack the field will crowd. Christian McCaffrey is Questionable (calf) as of Wednesday.
  • DET@MIA (50.2, No. 3): 31% Detroit blowout, with four chalk players in the game. Jahmyr Gibbs at 25% is the obvious play. Jared Goff with Jameson Williams (8%) is the quieter way in, and Amon-Ra St. Brown is Questionable (ribs).

Stacks to build

1. Dak Prescott + CeeDee Lamb + George Pickens (DAL)

  • Numbers: 53.6 points on average, an 80.4-point ceiling, and 15% of 10,000 sims clear 75. It costs $21,100, projected at 10%, 21% and 13% owned.
  • Bring-back + game flow: Jonathan Taylor (9%) lifts the four-player ceiling to 98.1. It hits when Indianapolis keeps pace and Dallas keeps throwing after halftime.
  • Why it wins a GPP: it's the best stack on the slate, and the field knows it: Dak and Lamb are both chalk. For leverage, swap in Ryan Flournoy (4%), who still clears 75 in 13% of sims and saves $1,500. Risk: Pickens is Questionable (hamstring) as of Wednesday.

2. Matthew Stafford + Puka Nacua + Tyler Higbee (LAR)

  • Numbers: 52.3 average, a 77.8 ceiling, 12% to clear 75, $18,700. Stafford projects at 5%, Nacua 25%, Higbee 3%.
  • Bring-back + game flow: Stefon Diggs (5%) takes the four-player ceiling to 90.4. Washington has to answer and push the Rams past their 26.6 team total.
  • Why it wins a GPP: Nacua is the top-owned receiver, but Stafford and Higbee are nearly empty, so the trio stays unique. Stafford and Nacua score together more than any other QB and receiver pair in our sims. Risk: Kyren Williams (12%) takes the touchdowns.

3. Tyler Shough + Chris Olave + Devaughn Vele (NO)

  • Numbers: 45.4 average, a 69.4 ceiling, 6% to clear 75, $18,200. Shough projects at 2%, Olave 10%, Vele 3%.
  • Bring-back + game flow: Quinshon Judkins (8%) gets the four-player ceiling to 83.9. The Saints are 3-point home favorites in a dome, and the game needs to run well over its 42.4 total.
  • Why it wins a GPP: Shough lands in the optimal lineup in 5.6% of sims against 2% projected ownership, and at $5,700 he leaves room for two stud backs. Risk: it's the lowest total of our three stacks.

What BirdieBuild says

  • Game totals matter: 50% of 169 Milly winners (k = 85) stacked a game totaled 48 or more, vs 27% of 2,639 games. Dallas (51.7) and the Rams game (48.2) clear that bar; New Orleans doesn't.
  • Favorites win: 70% of winners (k = 118) stacked a favored QB, vs 50% of 5,278 team-games, and all three of our QBs are favored. 17% of winners (k = 29) brought it back with the opposing RB, like Taylor.

Matchups to exploit

Our matchup numbers run through Week 5 and are weak (reliability under .25 at every position, .06 for WR1s), so use them as tie-breakers.

  • Matthew Stafford: Washington allows the second-most DK points to QBs (25.4 per game over 5 games), a +0.7-point edge. Tyler Higbee gets the same +0.7 against the defense allowing the most to TEs.
  • Malik Willis: +0.8 against Detroit over 5 games, the biggest QB edge on the slate, but he's already chalk at 13% (18% in single entry).
  • Tyler Warren: −0.4 against a Dallas defense allowing the fewest DK points to TEs (3.1 per game over 5 games). One more reason to skip him at 11%.

Punts

  • Evan Engram (TE, $2,800): a 14.7-point ceiling at 1% projected owned, the cheapest TE with real upside.
  • Dalton Schultz (TE, $3,200): a 16.5-point ceiling at 2%. Our ownership model tends to under-call value plays like these two when news hits, so expect both to climb.
  • Tyler Higbee (TE, $3,700): a 17.5-point ceiling at 3%, and already in our second stack.
  • Eagles (DST, $2,500): a 14.9-point ceiling at 8% and the best value at the position (3.05 points per $1K), against a chalk Giants defense at 14%.

Chalk we'll eat / chalk we'll fade

Eat

  • George Kittle (16%): $6,000 with a 34.4-point ceiling. He's in our optimal lineup 27% of the time, well above his ownership.
  • Puka Nacua (25%, 28% in single entry): his 38.7-point ceiling is the highest of any receiver, and his 22% optimal rate nearly matches his ownership.

Fade

  • Greg Dulcich (20%, 30% in single entry): a 15.7-point ceiling and only a 3.9% optimal rate. Take Evan Engram (1%) or Dalton Schultz (2%) instead.
  • Jonah Coleman (19%): he plays chalkier in single entry at 34%, with a 17.0-point ceiling and a 2.5% optimal rate. Pay up for Rhamondre Stevenson ($6,100): 8% owned, 16.6% optimal.

Bottom line
