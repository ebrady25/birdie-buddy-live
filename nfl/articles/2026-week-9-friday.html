Week 9 final call: Dallas is the best stack, and the field knows it

Fri Nov 6, 2026 · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-11-06 22:24 UTC

Dallas at Indianapolis has the slate's highest total at 51.9, and Prescott, Lamb and Pickens clear 75 DK points together in 14.9% of our 10,000 sims, the best rate of any stack. The field sees it too: Lamb projects at 20% owned and Prescott at 10%. We build it anyway and get different with the pieces around it.

TL;DR

  • Stack Prescott with Lamb and Pickens: they clear 75 DK points together in 15% of our sims, the best rate on the slate.
  • Bring the Dallas stack back with Jonathan Taylor, projected at just 4% owned.
  • Eat Jonah Coleman, whose projection jumped to 14.4 points since Wednesday; his price still earns the chalk.
  • Fade Malik Willis at 16% projected ownership and take Bo Nix, who has the same ceiling at a fraction of the crowd.
