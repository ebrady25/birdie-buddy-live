How we build these numbers

  • Line and game flows: de-vigged prediction-market prices (Polymarket + Kalshi). Blowout = favorite wins by 14+, Fav wins = by 4–13, Close = within 3, Upset = underdog wins by 4+.
  • Projections, ceilings and optimal %: our showdown simulator plays the game out in each of the four game flows, weighted by their market chances. Ceiling = about a 90th-percentile game; captain ceiling = 1.5x. Optimal % = how often a player is in the highest-scoring possible team, as captain or FLEX.
  • Ownership: our own model, trained on DraftKings showdown results, shown as projected % for large-field contests. It ranks players well, but its levels are rough, and it under-calls late, news-driven value.
  • The codex: our study of DraftKings showdown winning lineups (2018–25). Winner rates are shown with k and n.
