SNF showdown: a 29% Chicago blowout chance puts Williams at captain

Sun Nov 8, 2026 · TB @ CHI, kickoff Sun 8:20 PM ET · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-11-08 19:20 UTC

Chicago wins by 14 or more in 29% of market outcomes, tied for the most likely flow, and Caleb Williams scores most there. The crowd knows it: he projects at 63% FLEX ownership and 18% at captain, yet our sims make him the optimal captain even more often, 23%.

TL;DR

  • Captain Caleb Williams: his 23% captain optimal rate beats his 18% projected captain ownership.
  • Fade D'Andre Swift at FLEX: 47% projected owned but in only 35% of our sims' optimal teams.
  • Bucky Irving is the Tampa Bay captain for the 20% Upset path, at 11% projected captain ownership.

The game

Chicago is a 6-point favorite in a deep market, with a 48-point total split 27.0 to 20.9. Blowout and Fav wins each land 29% of the time, Close 22%, and a Tampa Bay Upset 20%. At 46 degrees with 7.5 mph wind, weather doesn't change a build.

Captain picks

1. Caleb Williams (CHI, QB)

  • Why: A 21.2-point projection and a 49.7-point captain ceiling, the best in the game. He climbs to 23.4 in a Chicago blowout and still projects 17.6 in an Upset.
  • Crowd: 18% projected captain ownership against a 23% captain optimal rate, the best leverage of any captain. Risk: Chicago runs the clock out on the ground and his passing volume dries up.

2. Colston Loveland (CHI, TE)

  • Why: A 44.5-point captain ceiling at $11,700, nearly Swift's ceiling for $3,300 less. His 14.8 projection barely moves across flows, from 13.3 to 15.7.
  • Crowd: 10% projected captain ownership, 13% captain optimal. BirdieBuild's study of 41 showdown winners in games with a 3.5-6.5 point spread: 59% (k = 24) captained someone from the favorite, but only 15% (k = 6) a tight end. That's the risk.

3. Bucky Irving (TB, RB)

  • Why: A 16.0-point projection and a 44.2-point captain ceiling. He projects 20.3 in an Upset and 17.5 when it stays Close.
  • Crowd: 11% projected captain ownership vs 13% captain optimal. Risk: a Chicago blowout drops him to 12.9.

Chalk we'll eat / chalk we'll fade

Eat

  • Caleb Williams at a projected 63% FLEX ownership is in nearly two of every three lineups, and he lands in 62% of optimal teams. The field is right; pay the price.

Fade

  • D'Andre Swift at FLEX: 47% projected owned, 35% optimal, the widest gap among the chalk. Pivot to Baker Mayfield, 38% projected FLEX ownership and 44% optimal; 41% of BirdieBuild winners (k = 17) used both QBs.
  • Emeka Egbuka: 26% projected owned, 9% optimal, and Questionable with a 64% chance to play. Pivot to Rome Odunze, 25% owned and 27% optimal.

Bottom line

We start with Williams at captain: the highest ceiling in the game, and still leverage as the chalk. The pivot is Irving, who owns the Upset and Close outcomes that Chicago-heavy builds ignore. Before lock, check Kyle Monangai, Questionable at a 52% chance to play; if he sits, Swift's workload grows, but the call stays Williams.
