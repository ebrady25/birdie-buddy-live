Week 8 Sunday update: A.J. Brown downgrade reshapes New England

Sun Nov 1, 2026 · BirdieBuddy NFL · numbers as of 2026-11-01 15:10 UTC

A.J. Brown is the change that matters most. He stays Questionable, but his chance to play fell from 79% to 34% overnight, and his projection dropped from 10.1 to 3.8 points. New England's 4 PM game now runs through the backfield and the tight end.

TL;DR

  • Stack Joe Burrow with Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins: still our top stack, clearing 75 points in 19% of 10,000 sims.
  • Hold Rhamondre Stevenson in FLEX: his projection is up to 15.7 as New England's backfield and receiver room thin out.
  • Fade Bhayshul Tuten at 24% projected ownership; the field piled on him since Friday.

News since Friday

  • A.J. Brown (NE): Questionable, now 34% to play as of this morning; projection 10.1 → 3.8. Hunter Henry and Rhamondre Stevenson absorb the work.
  • Carnell Tate and Tyjae Spears (TEN): reported Out → Out; Tate's projection 3.9 → 0. Xavier Restrepo rises from 0 to 3.5, and Elic Ayomanor gets a cleaner path to targets.
  • Brian Robinson Jr. (ATL): reported Out → Out. Khalil Herbert moves from 0 to 3.7 behind Bijan Robinson.
  • Emeka Egbuka (TB): reported Out → Out. Chris Godwin Jr. at $4,800 and 4% projected owned is the cheap pivot.
  • RJ Harvey (DEN): reported Out → Out (injured list). Jonah Coleman projects 10.5 at $4,300.
  • Breece Hall (NYJ) fell to 52% to play (projection 8.2 → 5.3). Jakobi Meyers (JAX) fell to 35% and needs news (7.9 → 4.2). If Meyers sits, Parker Washington (15.4, 12% owned) gains.

Movers

The ownership moves matter more than the projection moves. Chase Brown jumped from 19% to 30% projected owned, and Hunter Henry doubled from 7% to 15%. Henry is at 24% in single entry, so he plays chalkier there. Kendrick Bourne made the same move, 9% to 14% (22% in single entry).

Jordan Mason fell from 11% to 3% projected owned with a flat 8.5 projection. Lamar Jackson slid from 10% to 6% and Puka Nacua from 16% to 13%, so both get cheaper to own.

The top seven stacks didn't change. Josh Allen with Khalil Shakir and Dalton Kincaid is new at No. 8, clearing 75 in 7% of sims. Matthew Stafford is now our preferred late QB: an 18.6 projection at 4% projected owned for a 26.3-point team total. Drake Maye drops off our board with the Brown downgrade.

No game total or spread moved a full point.
