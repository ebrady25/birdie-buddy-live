8Before you lock

  • Inactives post about 90 minutes before each kickoff: 11:30 AM for the 1 PM games, 2:35 PM for the 4:05 PM games, 2:55 PM for the 4:25 PM games (ET). Check them before you submit.
  • Injury tags: Amon-Ra St. Brown (DET WR, questionable), George Pickens (DAL WR, questionable), Malik Nabers (NYG WR, questionable), Saquon Barkley (PHI RB, questionable), Hunter Henry (NE TE, questionable). If one sits, his teammates pick up the work and get more popular.
  • Late swap: 4 games kick off later (LV @ SF, GB @ NE, HOU @ LAC, ARI @ SEA). Put one of those players in your FLEX spot so you can still swap him if news breaks.
  • Quick checks: your QB has at least 1 of his own pass-catchers, no more than 3 players from one team, and you used at least $49,500 of the $50,000.
  • Lineups lock player by player as each game kicks off; the first lock is 1:00 PM ET.
