74 PM late-swap tips

The 4 PM games

  • LAC@LAR4:05 PM · total 45.5
  • KC@DEN4:25 PM · total 40.5
  • NE@MIA4:25 PM · total 43.0
  • Your 1 PM players lock at 1 PM. Players in the 4 PM games stay swappable until their own kickoff, even after the early games start.
  • Put a 4 PM player in your FLEX spot when you can. FLEX takes any RB, WR or TE, so a late scratch there can be swapped for almost anyone still unlocked.
  • Inactives for the 4 PM games post about 90 minutes before kickoff, around 2:35 to 2:55 PM ET. Check them before your 4 PM players lock.
  • If a 4 PM player is ruled out, swap to a 4 PM player at a similar salary, ideally the teammate who takes his work.
