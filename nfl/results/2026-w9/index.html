How we grade

  • Projections: our projection for a player who plays, against his actual DraftKings points. MAE is the average miss in points; bias is projection minus actual (positive = we were high). In range = share of fantasy-relevant players (projected 8+) who finished inside our p10-p90 range, which should land near 80%.
  • Ownership: the ownership we published before lock against actual %Drafted, averaged over DraftKings classic contests of each type (single entry, and large-field GPPs). Rank corr is the Spearman rank correlation; chalk MAE is the miss on players who were actually 10%+ owned.
  • Stacks, quarterbacks and builds: exactly what we published before lock (for weeks before our slate page, our models' final pre-lock output), scored with actual points. The top-1% line is the average 99th-percentile score across those contests.
  • Every number here comes from our own models. Nothing is edited after the games.
