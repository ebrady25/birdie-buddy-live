1. The short version

  • To make an account we need your email and a password. A name is optional.
  • We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.
  • We count page views and tool use with our own first-party analytics. We don't use cookies for it or ad trackers, and it switches off if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • Files you drop into our tools (like DraftKings salary files or contest results) are read in your browser and never uploaded.
  • Card payments will go through Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Email support@birdiebuddy.io any time to see or delete your data.

This summary is here to help. The full policy below is what counts.

3. What stays on your device

A lot of BirdieBuddy runs right in your browser, and that data never reaches us:

  • Files you load into our tools, such as DraftKings salary files, projection exports, lineup files and contest-standings files. They're read in your browser and not uploaded.
  • Your tool settings and saved work, such as Lineup Lab lineups, Classic and Showdown inputs, contest settings, and the golf watchlist or bookmarks.
  • A DraftKings username you type into the contest review, used only to find your entries in a file on your own device.

This is kept in your browser's local storage (see section 6) and stays there until you clear it.

4. How we use it

  • To run your account and membership: sign-in, members-only pages, discount codes and the members' Discord role.
  • To bill you and manage renewals and cancellations, once checkout is live.
  • To send you service emails, like account confirmations, receipts, renewal reminders, and notices about changes to our Terms or prices. If we ever send marketing emails, each one will have an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To understand which pages and tools get used, so we can make them better. We look at this in total, not to profile you.
  • To keep the Service secure, stop account sharing and abuse, and follow the law.

We don't sell your personal information, we don't "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't show targeted ads.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

Cookies. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may set strictly necessary security cookies to protect the site from bots and attacks. If we later move members-only checks to our server, we'll use one first-party cookie that holds your sign-in token, only so members-only pages load for you.

Local storage. Instead of cookies, the site saves a few things in your browser's local storage (and session storage, which clears when you close the tab):

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in, plus your name, email and membership status so pages load quickly.
  • A discount code you entered, until it's applied to your account.
  • Your tool settings, saved lineups and other work (see section 3).
  • Small display preferences, like how many rows a table shows.
  • The analytics tab ID and your analytics on/off setting.

You can clear all of this anytime in your browser settings. Clearing it signs you out and removes your saved tool work on that device.

Turning analytics off

Our analytics is first-party: events go only to our own database, never to an analytics or ad company, and we don't fingerprint your device. It switches itself off automatically if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. You can also turn it off for this browser here:

Checking this browser's setting…

The setting applies from the next page you open, on this browser only. Clearing your browser's site data resets it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account info: for as long as your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except anything we have to keep (below).
  • Billing records: for as long as tax and accounting laws require, which can be several years. Stripe keeps its own records under its policy.
  • Usage analytics: up to 13 months, then deleted. If you delete your account, events linked to it are detached from you.
  • Discord link: until you ask us to unlink it or delete your account.
  • Emails with us: for as long as we need them to help you and keep a record of the conversation.

Deleted data can stay in our providers' backups for a short time until those backups are replaced.

8. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See the personal information we have about you, or get a copy.
  • Correct something that's wrong.
  • Delete your account and personal information.
  • Unlink your Discord account.

Email support@birdiebuddy.io from the email address on your account (so we know it's you). We'll reply within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why. If we turn down a request, we'll explain, and you can reply to ask us to reconsider. You can also cancel your membership in Account and turn off analytics as described above. Using these choices will never change your price or how we treat you.

9. State privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with privacy laws, you may have legal rights over your personal information. These can include the right to know what we collect and how we use and share it, and to access, correct and delete it, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or its use for targeted advertising, the right to appeal our decision on a request, and the right not to be treated differently for using these rights. You may also be able to use an authorized agent to make a request for you; we may ask the agent for proof of permission and ask you to confirm your identity.

We don't sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, so there's nothing to opt out of there, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out anyway. We honor access, correction and deletion requests from all users, whether or not a particular state law applies to us. To make a request or an appeal, email support@birdiebuddy.io.

10. Children

BirdieBuddy is for adults 18 and older and isn't meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable safeguards: encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored only as secure hashes by our sign-in provider, database rules that let each member read only their own account record, and payments handled entirely by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

12. Users outside the US

BirdieBuddy is built for users in the United States. Our providers process and store data in the United States, so if you use the Service from elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in the US, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live.
