Get your file in three steps

  1. Open your history. On DraftKings, on a computer: DraftKings → My Contests → History.
  2. Download it. Download your entry history (CSV). It lists every contest you've entered: no lineups, no account details.
  3. Drop it here. Drag the file onto the box above, or tap Choose your CSV. Everything is worked out on your device.
